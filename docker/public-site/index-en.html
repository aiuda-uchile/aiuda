About the Project

What is Aiuda?

Aiuda assists educators in producing and enhancing educational materials using artificial intelligence.

It enables users to transcribe, translate, and subtitle content, analyze presentations, and generate accessibility recommendations. Its approach is clear: it does not replace teaching staff or automate pedagogical decisions.

It guides, suggests, and highlights. Academic and instructional judgment remains with the educators.

It operates entirely within each university’s infrastructure, processing materials locally without sending data to external services.

How is Aiuda used?

Three simple steps to make educational materials more accessible:

  1. Upload your materialPresentations, videos, or audio files remain within your institution’s infrastructure.

The People Behind the Project

Our Team

Behind every great project lies a story, and ours began in the classroom.

It was born from watching dedicated instructors spend hours adapting their course materials, and from seeing the frustration of students who—due to visual, auditory, or learning barriers—were left behind in classes designed for the majority, but not for everyone.

The true inspiration for Aiuda came from understanding an undeniable reality: inclusion is not hindered by a lack of will, but by a lack of time and accessible tools. We saw exceptional educators wanting to caption their instructional videos, only to be held back by complex software that devoured their personal time.

We saw the desire to layout accessible PowerPoint (PPT) presentations—with proper contrast, legible fonts, and appropriate alt-text—without knowing where to start amid overwhelming pedagogical theory.

We asked ourselves: how can we support faculty in including every student without tripling their workload?

The answer was building a technological bridge. An AI partner that automates the complex tasks: subtitling videos in minutes and adjusting or fixing slide presentations with a single click, ensuring full compliance with universal accessibility standards.

This project was not created merely to comply with accessibility regulations, but to restore valuable time to educators and safeguard every student’s right to learn. We want accessibility to cease being an “extra effort” and become the natural standard in every classroom.

We invite you to learn more about our journey and join us in fostering an educational environment where no student is left behind.
